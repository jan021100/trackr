# Trackr Study

## Scope and platform
Existing SvelteKit web app, desktop and mobile. This brief covers Study only. Other Trackr areas remain independent.

## User and purpose
German doctor in Prague, English medical programme. Surgery and Paediatrics passed; retain both as frozen read-only archives. Active study is now Czech, refreshing A1/A2 for everyday and clinical communication.

## Workflow
Short practice, copy a tutor prompt, track active time and pauses, import structured evidence and specific gaps, revisit weak skills and export gaps for Anki. Listening, speaking, reading and writing remain separate; no exam countdown or claimed CEFR certification.

## Constraints
Keep existing dark Study identity, account-owned storage and phone usability. Archive data loads only on explicit archive visits. Preserve all previous records; no data migration or deletion.
